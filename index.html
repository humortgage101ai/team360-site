What it does

Built for adult rec hockey. Not adapted for it.

Part-timers, spares, drop-in fees, deposit schedules, goalies who play free — the things that make beer league different are the things this was built around.

📅

Sign-ups that close themselves

Full-timers are in by default. Part-timers and spares get a window that opens before each game, with a waitlist once the roster fills. Nobody has to count.

📋

Line builder

Drag players into forwards, defence and goalie. Publish it and the whole team sees where they're playing. Captains can build it too, without admin access.

💰

Money that tracks itself

Season deposits with due dates, spare fees charged automatically when someone plays, partial payments that cascade to the next instalment, and a dated record of every one.

🔔

Notifications people act on

Sign-ups opening, lineups posted, spots freeing up, results. Sent to phones, not to a group chat where they scroll away in an hour.

📊

Stats, and who actually played

Goals, assists, penalty minutes and games played — with playoff eligibility tracked against your league's minimum, so there are no surprises in week ten.

🏆

Standings and season history

Pull your league standings in automatically. Roll into a new season and every player's record carries forward — including their player of the game awards.

Questions

The things captains actually ask.

Do my players need to download anything?

No. They open a link, enter the team code and password once, and it works like an app. On a phone they can add it to the home screen, which we'd recommend — that's also what lets notifications work on an iPhone.

How do players sign in?

Last name and jersey number. No accounts to create, no passwords to forget, no email addresses to collect. The team shares one code, which you send them once.

Does it handle payments?

It tracks them — who owes what, when it's due, what's been paid and when. It doesn't process the money itself, so you keep collecting however you already do, whether that's e-transfer or cash. Every payment is recorded with a date and who entered it.

What happens when the season ends?

You start the next one and the roster carries over, with each player's stats frozen into their history. Anyone who still owes a deposit stays owing it — the money follows the season it was collected for, not the season it was collected in.

What if I already use a league site?

Most leagues give you a schedule and standings and nothing else. Team360 handles the part they don't — who's playing, what the lines are, and who's paid. If your league uses SportNinja, standings pull in automatically.

What happens after 60 days if I don't pay?

Nothing gets deleted. The team goes read-only until you subscribe, and you can export the whole season to a file at any point, subscribed or not.

Is my team's data private?

Each team's data is walled off from every other team at the database level. Within a team, everyone shares one login, so anyone on your roster can see team information — rosters, schedules, who owes what. Treat it as visible to your team, because it is.
